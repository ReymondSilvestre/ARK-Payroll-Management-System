# Payslip: late and undertime moved to the EARNINGS side (as a minus)

- Late and undertime are no longer under DEDUCTIONS. They are negative lines under EARNINGS, below the absences: "Less: Late (1:00)", "Less: Undertime (1:00) - no deduction" (staff), "Less: Early out / undertime (0:30)" (teachers).
- Each line shows the dates and minutes in small print under its name (same detail that used to be on the deductions side).
- GROSS PAY on the payslip = earnings minus absences, late and undertime. TOTAL DEDUCTIONS no longer includes them.
- NET PAY is unchanged (the same amount comes out of gross and out of deductions).
- Staff undertime has no peso amount in the payroll computation (it was already "no deduction"), so it shows as P0.00 and is for information only.
- Teachers: Teaching pay still shows the full scheduled hours; late and early out are then taken off as minus lines, so gross = actual hours taught x rate.
- Display only: the Payroll page, reports and saved runs still keep gross / total deductions as before. Tell me if those should follow the payslip too.
