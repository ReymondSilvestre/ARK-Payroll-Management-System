# Payslip: absences moved from DEDUCTIONS to EARNINGS (as a minus)

- The Half day and Absences / unpaid leave lines are no longer under DEDUCTIONS. They are on the EARNINGS side as negative lines: "Less: Absences" -P645.00, "Less: Half day ..." (below the other earnings).
- GROSS PAY on the payslip = earnings minus the absences. TOTAL DEDUCTIONS no longer includes them.
- NET PAY is unchanged (the same amount is taken out of gross and out of deductions).
- Late, undertime, holiday / WFH deductions, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, tax, loans and cash advance stay under DEDUCTIONS.
- Teachers (paid per hour) have no absence deduction, so their payslip does not change.
- Display only: the Payroll page, reports and saved runs still keep gross / total deductions as before (absences inside deductions). Tell me if those should follow the payslip too.
